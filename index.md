## About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering at UC Berkeley, where I am a part of the [Co-Design Lab](https://codesign.berkeley.edu/) advised by Prof. Kosa Goucher-Lambert. I am closely associated with the [Jacobs Institute of Design](https://jacobsinstitute.berkeley.edu/), where I have supported design education through teaching and mentorship. Before Berkeley, I earned my BS in Mechanical Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin, where I conducted research in COVID-19 risk modelling under the supervision of Prof. Michael Lachmann and Prof. Jennifer Johnson-Leung. 

I study and design human-AI collaboration systems, particularly in complex multi-human settings. My research combines UX research, computational evaluation, and design theory to understand how people use, adopt, and collaborate with AI systems. During my PhD, I have worked with collaborators at City University of Hong Kong, Autodesk Research, Carnegie Mellon, and Virginia Tech on projects spanning GenAI-supported UX storyboarding, LLM evaluation for design, and computational simulation of design processes. Additionally, I have worked as a research intern at NASA Langley Research Center where I studied how professional designers adopted GenAI in real-world innovation settings and at HP Inc. where I designed AI conversational agents that collaborated with human teams.

Alongside research, I teach and mentor. I co-created a graduate-level design course, Human-AI Design Methods, and have mentored master’s and undergraduate students on research and design projects. I enjoy advising students who are interested in human-AI interaction research and want to pursue graduate education. If you are a UC Berkeley student and are interested, please feel free to reach out. Outside of research, I enjoy breakdancing, hiking, and reading.
